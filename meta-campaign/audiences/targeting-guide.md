# Audience Targeting Guide — Ooustream Campaign

---

## Campaign 1: AWARENESS

### Ad Set 1: Broad Cord Cutters
- **Age**: 25-54
- **Gender**: All
- **Location**: United States, United Kingdom, Canada
- **Interests**: Cord cutting, Streaming media, Smart TV, Over-the-top media service, Roku, Amazon Fire TV, Apple TV, Google Chromecast, Netflix, Hulu, Disney+
- **Behaviors**: Technology early adopters, Online shoppers
- **Exclude**: Existing customers (Custom Audience)
- **Placements**: Feed (FB + IG), Reels, Stories
- **Budget**: $20/day
- **Optimization**: Video Views (ThruPlay)

### Ad Set 2: Sports Fanatics
- **Age**: 21-55
- **Gender**: All (skew 65% male)
- **Location**: United States, United Kingdom, Canada
- **Interests**: NFL, NBA, Premier League, UFC, MLB, NHL, ESPN, Fox Sports, beIN Sports, Sky Sports, Sports bars, Fantasy football, Sports betting
- **Behaviors**: Engaged shoppers, Sports event attendees
- **Exclude**: Existing customers
- **Placements**: Feed (FB + IG), Reels, Stories
- **Budget**: $25/day
- **Optimization**: Video Views (ThruPlay)

### Ad Set 3: Tech Enthusiasts + Device Buyers
- **Age**: 22-45
- **Gender**: All
- **Location**: United States, United Kingdom, Canada
- **Interests**: Consumer electronics, Tech gadgets, Smart home, Android TV, Streaming devices, HDMI, 4K TV, Home theater, Gadget reviews, Unboxing
- **Behaviors**: Technology early adopters, Consumer electronics purchasers
- **Exclude**: Existing customers
- **Placements**: Feed (FB + IG), Reels, Right Column
- **Budget**: $15/day
- **Optimization**: Link Clicks

## Campaign 2: CONSIDERATION

### Ad Set 1: Retargeting — Engaged Users
- **Custom Audience**: Video viewers (50%+ watched) from Awareness campaign, last 60 days
- **+ Custom Audience**: IG/FB page engagers, last 90 days
- **Exclude**: Website purchasers
- **Placements**: Feed (FB + IG), Stories
- **Budget**: $15/day
- **Optimization**: Traffic (Landing Page Views)

### Ad Set 2: Retargeting — Website Visitors
- **Custom Audience**: Website visitors (all pages), last 30 days
- **Exclude**: Purchasers, Add-to-cart (last 3 days — let conversion campaign handle hot leads)
- **Placements**: Feed (FB + IG), Stories, Audience Network
- **Budget**: $15/day
- **Optimization**: Traffic (Landing Page Views)

---

## Campaign 3: CONVERSION

### Ad Set 1: Hot Leads
- **Custom Audience**: Add-to-cart, last 14 days
- **+ Custom Audience**: Website visitors (pricing/product page), last 7 days
- **Exclude**: Purchasers
- **Placements**: Feed (FB + IG), Stories
- **Budget**: $20/day
- **Optimization**: Conversions (Purchase)

### Ad Set 2: Pro Upgrade — Existing Users
- **Custom Audience**: Customer list (Basic plan users)
- **+ Custom Audience**: App users (active last 30 days)
- **Exclude**: Pro subscribers
- **Placements**: Feed (FB + IG), Stories, Reels
- **Budget**: $10/day
- **Optimization**: Conversions (Purchase)

---

## Exclusion Lists (Critical)

- **Always exclude**: Existing purchasers from Awareness + Consideration campaigns
- **Always exclude**: Employees / team members (create custom audience)
- **Consider excluding**: Under 21 (low purchase intent for streaming hardware)

---

## Dayparting Recommendations

- **Sports Ads**: Increase budget 2x on weekends (Sat-Sun) and game days
- **General Awareness**: Even distribution, slight increase evenings (6-10 PM local)
- **Conversion**: Even distribution — don't restrict, let Meta optimize

## Geographic Notes

- **U.S.**: Primary market, largest cord-cutting population
- **U.K.**: Strong secondary market (Sky TV cord cutters)
- **Canada**: Growing cord-cutting trend, Rogers/Bell defectors
- **Expand to**: Australia, Germany, Netherlands once initial campaigns prove ROI
