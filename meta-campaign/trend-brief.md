## IPTV Advertising on Meta — Key Considerations

Meta's policies restrict direct IPTV service promotion. Successful campaigns in this space:
- **Focus on the app/service** (Ooustream) rather than specific content/channels
- **Promote the app experience** — UI, features, ease of use, $20/mo value
- **Avoid mentioning specific networks or channels** — frame as "live TV" generically
- **Lead with the cord-cutting lifestyle** — savings ($20/mo vs $150+/mo cable), convenience, freedom
- **Lead with the 24-hour free trial** — zero-risk entry point converts browsers to subscribers
- **Use compliant language** — "streaming app", "entertainment hub", not "IPTV"

## Top 5 Viral Hooks to Adapt

1. **"Stop scrolling if you still pay for cable"** — Pattern interrupt + pain point
2. **"POV: You just cancelled cable and it only costs $20/month now"** — Aspirational transformation
3. **"This $20/month app replaced my entire cable box"** — Curiosity + value
4. **"I was today years old when I learned you could watch 4 games at once"** — Discovery/shock
5. **"My cable company is going to hate this video"** — Controversy + curiosity gap

## Top 3 Visual Styles for This Niche

1. **Dark Premium Showcase** — Matte black backgrounds, gold accents, product hero shot. Matches the Ooustream brand identity (#0A0A0A + #FFC107). Signals premium quality.
2. **UGC/Handheld Feel** — iPhone-native footage style, text overlay, casual typography. Outperforms polished studio content on Reels/Stories in 2026.
3. **Split Comparison** — Cable vs. Ooustream side-by-side. Red X / Green checkmarks. Price contrast. Extremely effective for consideration-stage retargeting.

## Top 3 Copy Frameworks

1. **PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solve)** — Cable bills are insane → you're paying for channels you never watch → Ooustream gives you everything for $20/mo
2. **Social Proof Stack** — Lead with star ratings, user count, testimonials. "50,000+ cord cutters switched"
3. **Before/After Transformation** — Life with cable (frustrated, expensive, $150+/mo) → Life with Ooustream ($20/mo, everything, MultiView)

## Audience Targeting Recommendations

### Primary Audiences
- **Cord Cutters**: Interest in Roku, Fire TV Stick, Apple TV, streaming services
- **Sports Fans**: Interest in NFL, NBA, Premier League, UFC, ESPN, Fox Sports
- **Tech Early Adopters**: Interest in smart home, gadgets, tech reviews

### Lookalike Strategy
- 1% lookalike from website purchasers (highest quality)
- 2% lookalike from add-to-cart events
- 3% lookalike from page engagers

### Retargeting Pools
- Website visitors (last 30 days) — exclude purchasers
- Video viewers (75%+ watched) — last 60 days
- IG/FB page engagers — last 90 days
- Add-to-cart abandoners — last 14 days
