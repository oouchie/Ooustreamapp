## 1. Campaign Overview

### Product
**Ooustream** — A premium streaming app that turns any Android TV or Fire Stick into a complete entertainment hub. Live TV, movies, series, and the industry-first MultiView feature (4 simultaneous live streams) — all in one app.

- **Price**: Starting at $20/month
- **Trial**: 24-hour free trial
- **Sign Up**: ooustick.com
- **Pro Upgrade**: ooustick.com/subscribe/pro
- **Logo**: TV icon with blue/cyan aurora waves + play button. Tagline: "Seamless Streaming Technology"

### Goals
- **Primary**: Drive Ooustream signups via ooustick.com ($20/mo subscription)
- **Secondary**: Convert existing Basic users to Pro plan (MultiView unlock)
- **Tertiary**: Build brand awareness in the cord-cutting space

### Hero Differentiator
**MultiView** — No mainstream streaming app lets you watch 4 live channels simultaneously on one screen. This is our unfair advantage, especially for sports fans.

### Brand Identity
- **Name**: Ooustream (app/service) | Ooustick (physical device, secondary reference)
- **Colors**: #0A0A0A (dark background), #FFC107 (gold accent), #00B0FF (aurora blue from logo), white text
- **Tone**: Premium but approachable. Confident, not corporate. Slightly rebellious (we're the cable killer).
- **Design Language**: "Aurora Cinema" — dark, cinematic, gold accents, clean typography
- **Key Messaging**: "$20/mo", "24-hour free trial", "Cancel anytime", "No contracts"

## 2. Trend Research Summary

### Key Findings
- **Reels dominate**: 50%+ of IG ads run on Reels. 9:16 vertical creative is mandatory.
- **UGC outperforms polished**: Authentic, iPhone-native-looking content converts better than studio quality.
- **3-second rule**: Users decide in 1.7 seconds (mobile) whether to engage. Hook is everything.
- **Cord-cutting is mainstream**: 77M+ U.S. households have cut cable. The market is massive and growing.
- **Meta restricts IPTV terms**: Frame as "streaming device" and "entertainment hub", never "IPTV service".

### Hooks That Work Right Now
1. "Stop scrolling if you still pay for cable"
2. "POV: You just cancelled cable"
3. "This tiny stick replaced my cable box"
4. "I was today years old when I learned..."
5. "My cable company is going to hate this"

### Visual Styles Converting
1. Dark premium product showcase (matches our brand perfectly)
2. UGC/casual text-overlay style
3. Before/after comparison splits

## 7. A/B Testing Plan

### Week 1-2: Creative Testing
- Run all ad variants within each ad set
- Let Meta's Advantage+ distribute budget to winners
- KPI: ThruPlay Rate (Awareness), CTR (Consideration), CPA (Conversion)

### Week 2-3: Identify Winners
- Pause ads with CTR < 0.8% (Awareness) or CPA 2x above target (Conversion)
- Double budget on top 2 performers per ad set
- Note which hooks, visuals, and copy structures win

### Week 3-4: Scale & Iterate
- Create new variants based on winning elements
- Test new audiences: Lookalike 1% from purchasers
- Increase daily budget 20% on winning ad sets

### KPIs to Track

| Metric | Awareness Target | Consideration Target | Conversion Target |
|--------|-----------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| CPM | < $15 | < $20 | < $25 |
| CTR | > 1.5% | > 2% | > 3% |
| CPC | < $1.50 | < $1.00 | < $2.00 |
| ThruPlay Rate | > 15% | — | — |
| Landing Page Views | — | > 60% of clicks | — |
| CPA (Purchase) | — | — | < $30 |
| ROAS | — | — | > 3x |

### Optimization Rules
- **Kill**: Any ad below 0.5% CTR after 1,000 impressions
- **Scale**: Any ad above 2x average CTR → increase budget 30%
- **Refresh**: Rotate creatives every 2-3 weeks to prevent ad fatigue
- **Audiences**: Test lookalikes after 100+ purchases in pixel data

## 8. Compliance Notes

### Meta Policy Considerations
- Frame Ooustream as a **streaming app/service**, not an "IPTV service"
- Promote the **app experience and features**, not specific content/channels
- Use "Live TV" generically — never mention specific networks
- Focus on **cord-cutting lifestyle** (savings, freedom, convenience)
- Lead with the **24-hour free trial** — low-risk entry point
- If ads get flagged, appeal with emphasis on streaming app subscription
- Keep ad accounts clean — one product per ad account

### Recommended Language
- DO say: "streaming app", "entertainment hub", "live TV", "movies and series", "cord cutting", "$20/month", "free trial"
- DON'T say: "IPTV", specific channel names, "free TV", "pirate", "unlocked"
